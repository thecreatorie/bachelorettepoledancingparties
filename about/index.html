About Us

About Bachelorette Pole Dancing Parties

At Pole Fitness Studio, we have five dance rooms. Two of the rooms cater to pole dancing parties for groups of all sizes celebrating bachelorette parties, birthday parties, girls night out and really any occasion you can think of.

We have celebrated with over 15,000 bachelorettes since we opened in Las Vegas in 2005. Fawnia Mondey, and her staff of 30 incredible instructors have developed super fun routines which have kept parties coming back year after year.

Yes you can request your instructor, because you are the boss when it comes to your pole dancing party. You can even tell us what music you'd like. Do you prefer to keep it classy and learn burlesque? Let us know! We can build the party around your interests.

Popular party style choices are pole dancing and chair dancing with a striptease, burlesque, pole only, chair and twerk only, yoga, or request a belly dancing party.

We will help you create lasting memories for your bride-to-be and her besties! We will also take lots of photos and videos of your party if you'd like.

Do you plan on going to a club or pool while in Vegas? We will set you up with free VIP entry for your entire group, any day, any night!

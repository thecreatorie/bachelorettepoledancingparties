Meet the Founder

Hi, I'm Fawnia

My name is Fawnia. I am a pole dance instructor with over 30 years experience performing and teaching. I was also a fitness model, personal trainer, writer, producer and actress. After 20 years of competing, I have hung up my figure competition suit, in exchange for being a mom, and nutrition coach.

I moved to Las Vegas from Canada in 2005 and have fallen in love with both the people, and the city. I will forever be grateful for my surprise career path shift after highschool, which steered from being a counselor or police officer, to exotic dancer. Teaching pole dancing to hundreds of thousands of people from all over the world these last 30 years has been an absolute blast.

Las Vegas tourists are my favorite. I love seeing you all dressed up for your bachelorette parties, birthdays, and girls night out events. I am looking forward to meeting you, the new generation of pole dancers. We are open for bachelorette parties 24/7 at Pole Fitness Studio!

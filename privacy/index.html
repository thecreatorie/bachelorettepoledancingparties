Privacy Policy

We keep it simple: we collect as little as possible, we never sell your information, and we only use what you share with us to plan your party.

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Who we are

Bachelorette Pole Dancing Parties (BPDP) is a small Las Vegas event business. Our parties take place at Pole Fitness Studio, 4265 S. Arville Street, Suite A, Las Vegas, Nevada 89103. You can reach us any time at 702-878-POLE (7653).

What we collect

We collect information in two ways:

  • Contact form. If you use the form on our contact page, we receive the details you type in — your name, email address, party date, group size, and message. The form is processed by Formspree, a third-party form service, which delivers your message to our inbox. We use this information only to answer your questions and plan your party.
  • Analytics cookies. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use our site — for example, which pages are popular. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects usage data such as pages viewed, approximate location, and device type. This data is aggregated and does not tell us who you are personally. You can block these cookies in your browser settings or with tools like the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Ever.
  • We do not send marketing emails unless you ask us to.
  • We do not collect payment details on this website.

Booking and third-party sites

Party bookings are handled on polefitnessstudio.com, our studio's website. When you book there, that site's own privacy practices apply. Links to other sites (such as Facebook or Google Reviews) are likewise governed by those companies' policies.

How long we keep your information

We keep contact messages only as long as we need them to organize your event or answer your question, then delete them in the normal course of business.

Your choices

Want to know what information we have about you, or want it deleted? Just call us at 702-878-POLE (7653) or send a note through the contact page and we will take care of it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
